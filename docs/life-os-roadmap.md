# Sakuna Life OS — next phases

Goal: one calm system for the whole 24-hour day. Every feature works together, the AI helps instead of adding
pressure, and the app learns a little more about you every day. We build **one phase at a time**, test it on the
phone, then start the next.

**Built so far:** tasks and the daily plan, goals and the points game, habits, Shield (blocking, Strict Mode,
Insights), flashcards and the mistake notebook, coach check-ins, buy list and wish list, the life balance page, the
document vault, and voice commands.

**Rules that stay the same in every phase:**

- The AI suggests and Sakuna never acts for you: it doesn't buy, sell, send or post anything.
- Times, money and points are worked out by plain code, so the app still works with no AI key.
- **No-stress mode.** Missing a step never triggers guilt messages. On a late day you get a short version
  (3 essentials), rest days are allowed, and a streak can be "frozen" instead of broken.

## Phase 1: My Day (routines and checklists) ✅ done

Turn the notebook into a day you can tick through on the phone.

- **Routines with time blocks.** Each routine is a list of steps you tick one by one, with points and a streak. Your
  notes are loaded already (appendix):
  - Morning, After gym 11:00–13:00, Office 18:00–23:00, Night 23:00–00:30
- **Step player.** Opens the next routine on time and shows one step at a time: tick, skip or "later". A gentle
  reminder comes if a routine hasn't started.
- **Short version.** Each routine has 3 must-do steps for busy days, for example Night: brush, medicine,
  no phone after 12.
- **Packing checklists.** Gym bag and office bag, ticked before leaving and reset every day. They can link to the buy
  list ("hair wax running low → add to buy list").
- **Medicine and supplement steps** (Mg, Zinc after meals, Tripala, vitamins) with reminders. Sakuna only reminds you;
  it gives no medical advice.
- **Meal card.** Shows your eating rules while eating (chew slowly, no phone, upright, stop at 80% full), then the
  0–10 and 10–20 minute after-meal steps. You can log a meal with "slow / rushed / phone".

## Phase 2: Focus engine ("1 hour, 1 task") ✅ done

Your notebook method, built into a focus session.

1. Set a clear target (2-minute timer) and pick **one** task from today's plan.
2. Kill distractions: Shield starts a quick block of your **Distractions** list.
3. Choose 50/10 or 25/5. "Just 5 minutes" start and a 5-4-3-2-1 countdown. Water and posture reminder.
4. Break card: drink water, walk, sunlight, eye rest, a short brain game.
5. 2-minute review: what got done and what's next, saved to the task and the plan.

- **Distractions list loaded into Shield:** Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Telegram, adult sites (built-in filter),
  WhatsApp. Plus a **bedtime schedule**: no phone after 00:00, which Strict Mode can lock.
- **"Avoid" habits for things an app can't block** (oversleeping, sugar or oily food, gossip, scrolling in bed).
  You log these honestly; the app never punishes.
- **Daily focus score** from Shield Insights: longest focus, pickups, and time in distracting apps.

## Phase 3: Exam coach (IELTS and Goethe German)

- Exam date, target band or level, and a study plan worked backwards from the date, placed into free time in the
  daily plan.
- **Skill tracking:** IELTS Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking; Goethe Lesen, Hören, Schreiben and Sprechen.
- Daily sessions using the existing flashcards (vocabulary, spaced repetition) and the mistake notebook.
- **Writing feedback:** paste or type an essay or letter and get corrections plus an **estimated** band or level,
  always labelled as an estimate.
- **Speaking practice by voice:** you answer a question out loud, get the transcript and tips.
- A log of mock-test scores with a trend chart, and a "ready by exam day?" forecast.

## Phase 4: AI workers (a small team)

Named helpers, each with one job. They run on a schedule and put their notes into one inbox, the **Today card**.

| Worker | Job |
|---|---|
| Morning coach | builds today's plan from routines, tasks, the exam plan and your energy |
| Focus guard | watches focus and Shield data, suggests a better time for deep work |
| Study coach | picks today's exam practice and revision from your mistakes |
| Night reviewer | short wrap-up: wins, what moved to tomorrow, one lesson |
| News scout | tech and AI news, and CSE market news (Phase 6) |
| Market analyst | your watchlist and portfolio checks (Phase 7) |

- Each worker has an on/off switch, a time, and a daily AI budget. With no AI key they still work, using plain rules.
- Workers only suggest. Anything that changes data goes through the existing approval levels.

## Phase 5: Learns you, day by day

**Why not fine-tuning:** retraining a model on your data is expensive, slow and bad for privacy. Instead Sakuna builds
a personal profile that updates every day:

- **Patterns:** your best focus hours, routines you often skip, sleep vs. next-day focus, distraction triggers.
- **A weekly "about you" report** in plain words, with one small change to try next week.
- **Feedback on advice:** 👍/👎 on every suggestion. Advice you find useful shows up more; the rest fades out.
- Preferences and facts go into the existing memory (second brain), which you can see and edit.
- **Answer caching:** repeated questions reuse earlier answers, which keeps the AI cost low.

## Phase 6: News and ideas digest

- One short daily digest: tech and AI, new tools worth learning, and Sri Lanka and CSE market news.
- Built from public news feeds (RSS) and summarised by the AI. Each item links to its source.
- "Save to second brain" and "make it a learning task" buttons.

## Phase 7: CSE investing assistant

- **Portfolio:** holdings, average cost in LKR, dividends, profit and loss, and how much of your money is in each
  company and sector.
- **Watchlist with your own rules:** target buy price, stop-loss, P/E and dividend-yield limits. Sakuna alerts you
  when a rule is hit, worked out by plain code.
- **Research notes:** the AI summarises company announcements and results, with pros, cons and risks.
- **Buy and sell signals come only from rules you set.** They're always labelled "ideas to check, not financial
  advice". Sakuna never places trades; you buy and sell through your broker.
- **Price data needs a legal source.** Before we build this phase, we check what the CSE allows us to use. Until then,
  you can type in prices by hand.
